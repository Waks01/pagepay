## 🛡️ Risk Mitigation

### **Fraud Prevention:**

✅ KYC verification (phone + NIN/BVN)  
✅ Escrow system (7-day hold for retention tasks)  
✅ Reputation scoring (better history = better tasks)  
✅ Registration fee (₦1,000 to prevent fake accounts)  
✅ Screenshot + AI + human review

### **Quality Assurance:**

✅ Retention checks (did user stay followed/joined?)  
✅ Automatic clawback if user violates  
✅ 3-strike policy (3 violations = suspension)  
✅ Sponsor satisfaction guarantees
