## 11. Fraud Prevention (Critical)

### Problems Competitors Face:

**1. Fake Engagement**

- Users create multiple accounts
- Use VPNs to appear as different people
- Submit fake screenshots

**2. Collusion**

- Users and sponsors work together to drain platform funds
- Sponsor creates campaign, their own fake accounts "complete" it, withdraw money

**3. Low-Quality Actions**

- Users unfollow immediately after getting paid
- Sponsors complain engagement doesn't last

### Solutions from SEHRR (Best Practice):

**1. KYC Verification**

- Verify email
- Verify phone number
- Link social accounts (can only link once)
- Photo ID for higher limits

**2. Task Review Process**

- Screenshot submission required
- Human review team checks each submission
- Automated checks (did the follow actually happen?)
- Delay payout by 24-48 hours (gives time to detect fraud)

**3. Retention Checks**

- Re-check after 7 days (did user unfollow?)
- If yes, user account gets flagged/banned
- Or sponsor gets partial refund

**4. Escrow System**

- Sponsor funds held in escrow
- Released to earners only after verification
- Refunded to sponsor if task not completed properly
