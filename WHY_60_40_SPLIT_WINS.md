# Why 60/40 Split is the Perfect Balance

**Date:** August 1, 2026  
**Decision:** Users get 60%, PagePay keeps 40%

---

## 🎯 The Three Options We Considered

### Option 1: 70/30 Split (Too Generous)

- Users earn: ₦7 per ₦10 task
- PagePay keeps: ₦3
- **Problem:** Only ₦2.58 net profit after ₦0.42 costs = 25.8% net margin
- **Risk:** Not enough cushion for infrastructure scaling, promotions, or unexpected costs

### Option 2: 50/50 Split (Industry Standard - Hawkit)

- Users earn: ₦5 per ₦10 task
- PagePay keeps: ₦5
- **Problem:** No competitive advantage for user acquisition
- **Risk:** Why would users switch from Hawkit if earnings are the same?

### Option 3: 60/40 Split ✅ (WINNER)

- Users earn: ₦6 per ₦10 task
- PagePay keeps: ₦4
- **Benefits:**
  - ✅ Users earn 20% MORE than Hawkit (₦6 vs. ₦5)
  - ✅ ₦5.58 net profit after ₦0.42 costs = 37.2% net margin
  - ✅ Room for infrastructure, growth, and promotions
  - ✅ Still beats all competitors on user earnings

## 🏆 Why 60/40 is the Sweet Spot

### 1. **Still Best-in-Market for Users**

- Hawkit (market leader): 50/50 (users get ₦5)
- Sidegig: 50/50 (users get ₦5)
- EarnIT: 40/60 (users get ₦4)
- **PagePay: 60/40 (users get ₦6)** ← BEST!

### 2. **Healthy Margins for Growth**

- 35.8% net margin is excellent for a marketplace
- Comparable to successful Nigerian fintech (Paystack ~30%, Flutterwave ~25%)
- Enough cushion for:
  - Server scaling as you grow to 100K users
  - SMS/push notification costs
  - 24/7 customer support team
  - Marketing and user acquisition
  - Referral bonuses
  - Volume discounts for sponsors

### 3. **Room for Promotions Without Losing Money**

**Example Promo: "First 100 Tasks, Earn 70%"**

- New user's first 100 tasks: You keep 30% instead of 40%
- Cost to you: ₦1 less profit × 100 tasks = ₦100 acquisition cost
- User earns ₦700 instead of ₦600 in first 100 tasks
- **Still profitable:** ₦3 - ₦0.42 costs = ₦2.58 net per task

With 70/30 split, you'd have no room for this.

### 4. **Sponsor Volume Discounts**

**Example: Bulk Discount for 10K+ Orders**

- Normal: 1,000 likes = ₦10,000
- Bulk: 10,000 likes = ₦90,000 (10% discount)
- User still earns: ₦6/like = ₦60,000 total
- Your margin drops from 40% to 33%
- **Still profitable:** ₦2.97 net per task

With 70/30 split, bulk discounts would make you unprofitable.

## 💡 Why NOT 70/30?

### Short-term thinking:

- Yes, users would earn ₦7 vs. ₦6 (17% more)
- Yes, user acquisition would be slightly easier

### Long-term problems:

1. **Thin margins kill growth:**
   - Can't afford aggressive marketing
   - Can't hire support team
   - Can't scale infrastructure
   - One bad month = losses

2. **No room for promotions:**
   - Can't do "double earnings weekend"
   - Can't offer referral bonuses
   - Can't give volume discounts
   - Locked into pricing forever

3. **Investor concerns:**
   - 25.8% net margin looks risky
   - Hard to justify valuation
   - Difficult to raise Series A

4. **The difference isn't meaningful to users:**
   - ₦7 vs. ₦6 = ₦1 difference per task
   - On 50 tasks/day = ₦50/day difference
   - Monthly = ₦1,500 difference
   - **But both are still way better than Hawkit's ₦5!**

---

## ✅ Final Verdict: 60/40 is Perfect

### The Math:

- **User side:** 20% better than market leader (Hawkit)
- **Business side:** 35.8% net margin (healthy and sustainable)
- **Growth potential:** Room for promotions, discounts, and scaling

### The Psychology:

- Users see "60%" and think "I keep most of it"
- Users compare ₦6 vs. Hawkit's ₦5 and switch immediately
- Sponsors see ₦10/action = same as Hawkit = no sticker shock

### The Strategy:

- Start with 60/40 to build healthy foundation
- Once you hit 50K+ users and have economies of scale...
- THEN you can consider moving to 65/35 or even 70/30
- But only when infrastructure costs per task drop significantly
