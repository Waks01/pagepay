**Market Size:**

- Every content creator needs views to trend
- Nigerian creators post 50-100 stories/day
- Demand: **MASSIVE**

**Verification:**

- Screenshot showing story view + reaction
- 24-hour escrow
- Auto-verified via OCR

- Critical first 5-10 minutes determines if live goes viral

**Use Cases:**

**Market Size:**

- Estimated 10,000+ live sellers active daily in Nigeria

- PagePay profit: 50 viewers × ₦15 × 16 streams = **₦12,000/month per seller**
- 1,000 active live sellers = **₦12M/month**

**Technical Requirements:**
