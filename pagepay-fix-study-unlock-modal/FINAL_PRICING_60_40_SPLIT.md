## 🎯 Executive Summary

After analyzing verified market data from Hawkit (Nigeria's leading task marketplace), PagePay will use a **60/40 revenue split**:

- **Users earn 60%** of every transaction (₦6 out of ₦10)
- **PagePay keeps 40%** for infrastructure, fraud prevention, and profit (₦4 out of ₦10)

**Competitive Position:**

- **Hawkit:** 50/50 split (users earn ₦5, platform keeps ₦5)
- **PagePay:** 60/40 split (users earn ₦6, platform keeps ₦4)
- **User advantage:** +20% more earnings on PagePay!
