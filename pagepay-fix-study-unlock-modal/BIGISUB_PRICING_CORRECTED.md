## 🔴 Why This Proves Bot Delivery

### 1. Uniform Pricing Across Complexity Levels

**Simple action (share):** ₦16.8  
**Complex action (page follow):** ₦16.8  
**Complex action (group join):** ₦16.8

With real human labor, complex actions would cost MORE, not the same. The fact that all three cost exactly ₦16.8 proves automated delivery.

### 2. Below Competitor User Payouts

**Bigisub charges sponsors:** ₦12.6 per like  
**EarnIT pays users:** ₦15-₦25 per like  
**Math:** Bigisub would lose ₦2.4-₦12.4 per transaction

**Bigisub charges sponsors:** ₦16.8 per follow  
**SEHRR pays users:** ₦50-₦100 per follow  
**Math:** Bigisub would lose ₦33.2-₦83.2 per transaction

**Conclusion:** IMPOSSIBLE to be using real Nigerian humans.

### 3. No Price Differentiation = No Quality Tiers

- ALL services in ₦6-₦62 range
- No "verified" or "premium" options
- No quality guarantees mentioned
- Consistent low pricing = consistent bot delivery
