📱 VTU Services Carousel - Design Specs

Placement
Inserted directly below "Keep Reading" horizontal carousel, before "Browse by Category" section.
Layout
Horizontal snap-scroll carousel matching the Keep Reading pattern. Each card is 140px wide with 12px gap. Shows 3-4 cards at a time on typical phone screens.
Card Design
Compact cards with emoji icon, service name, and earn rate badge. Each service has a unique icon background color for quick visual scanning.
Services Shown
Airtime, Data, Electricity, TV, Recharge Pin, Betting, ISP, Education (Result Checker), SMS — all 9 VTU services from Bigisub.
Interactions
Horizontal swipe to browse services. Tapping a card navigates to that service's buy screen. "See All" link could show full grid view.
Earn Rates
Badge shows approximate earn rate (from Bigisub commission). These are estimates — actual points are calculated after purchase based on real commission.
Conditional Display
Should this carousel: (a) show to everyone, (b) hide premium users, (c) only show free users? Currently shown to all.
