PagePay VTU Redesign

Visual mockup for the new Buy Data screen (buy-airtime, buy-electricity, buy-tv, buy-recharge-pin, buy-betting, buy-isp, buy-education, buy-sms follow the same pattern).

Design Decision 1 — Max 4 sections. The old screen had 7+ vertical sections (header, phone, network chips, amount chips, custom amount, earn card, pay button). The new design collapses this into 4 cards: Phone → Network → Plan → Summary & Pay. Each section is a self-contained card with clear internal spacing (8px grid).
Design Decision 2 — Segmented control for network. The horizontal scroll of chips (Mtn, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) is replaced with a tight segmented control. It reduces height by ~60% and communicates "pick one" more clearly. On smaller screens this prevents the chips from wrapping to a second row.
Design Decision 3 — Plan cards in a 2-column grid. Plans are shown in a 2-column grid instead of horizontal scroll or single-column list. This shows 2 plans at a time, making comparison easy without excessive scrolling. Each card shows the plantype tag (SME, GIFTING, etc.), size, price, and estimated points.
Design Decision 4 — Plantype filter tabs. Bigisub returns structured plantype values (SME, GIFTING, DataTransfer, CGIFTING) instead of generic daily/weekly/monthly buckets. The tabs reflect the actual categories from the API. Networks differ: MTN/GLO have all 4 types, Airtel has SME+GIFTING, 9mobile has DataTransfer only.
Design Decision 5 — Inline earn badge. The old repetitive earn notice card is replaced with a small inline badge that appears only after a plan is selected. It shows "+120 pts" without consuming a full card slot.
Design Decision 6 — Inline phone validation. The phone input shows a green checkmark inside the field once 11 digits are entered. Error text appears directly below the input with proper spacing. No more floating error text with negative margin.
Design Decision 7 — Visual hierarchy via section cards. Each section is visually isolated with a white card on the paper background. This creates clear grouping: "enter your number", "choose your network", "pick a plan", "pay now".
Design Decision 8 — Sticky CTA button. The pay button is placed at the bottom of the scroll view but feels anchored. It always shows context (selected plan + amount) so the user knows exactly what they're paying for before tapping.
Design Decision 9 — Real Bigisub validation with full data display. Each validate button calls Bigisub's verify endpoint and shows the actual returned fields on success:
  • Electricity: customer_name + address from POST /api/v2/bills/electricity/verify/
  • TV: customer_name + account_status from POST /api/v2/vtu/cable/verify/
  • Betting: customer_name + validation_reference + requires_validation_ref from POST /api/v2/betting/validate/ (uses customer_id field)
